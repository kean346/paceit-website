# SIGNAL — revised film direction

The first version was rejected for slide-like design, a dominant grid and weak camera choreography. V2 treats the software as the subject of a studio product film.

Palette: Carbon #090b0d, Emerald #43b19b, Silver #f3f4f0, Limestone #efefeb, Alert copper #f18c68. Space Grotesk carries the cinematic typography; Plus Jakarta Sans carries supporting copy and the actual website content.

Signature: an original sculpted emerald conduit, lit with broad white studio reflections. Its curve starts in macro, surrounds the mobile reveal and returns before the wordmark. The conduit is actual projected cylinder geometry with normal-based lighting. No repeating background grid or numbered slide labels.

Composition alternates macro object, large warning typography, three-quarter phone, full-frame desktop, travelling browser layers, light studio product panels, full-frame functional workflow, a connecting path, exploded analytics cards, and a held brand resolve. Portrait positions are separately composed.

The uploaded reference guides these changes in visual scale and scene choreography. Website imagery remains authentic. The film is an original graphic production; no reference footage or music is included. The design skill informed the stronger single visual motif, reduction of decorative labels, and frame-by-frame critique.

Production source: scripts/film-v2.cjs, scripts/tube-v2.cjs, scripts/analytics-v2.cjs and scripts/audio-v2.py. Old outputs remain available as the prior version; deliver only out/v2 files for this revision.
