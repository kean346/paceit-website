## Earned links and sharing

The homepage now has Open Graph/Twitter image metadata for clean shared links. Service descriptions also have stable deep links at `/#custom-software`, `/#business-automation` and `/#dashboards-reporting`. These are navigation targets, not additional sitemap pages.

The strongest proposed shareable asset is the original quotation-workflow checklist, followed by a factual SA Family Travel case study once approved. Publish useful text on accessible pages; provide a downloadable version only if it adds value. Ask real clients/partners for appropriate attribution links and maintain consistent genuine business listings where eligible. No purchased backlinks, automated outreach, fake citations or messages to third parties are part of this change.

## After deployment

1. Confirm `paceit.co.za` is connected as the primary HTTPS domain in Vercel. Redirect the www and production Vercel aliases only after the primary domain is working.
2. Verify the domain in Google Search Console (and Bing Webmaster Tools if desired), then submit `https://paceit.co.za/sitemap.xml`.
3. Inspect the homepage's indexed canonical and rendered content. Monitor real search queries, indexing, crawl errors and field Core Web Vitals.
4. Publish approved, substantive pages gradually and add them to `indexableRoutes`, with unique titles, descriptions, canonicals and contextual links.

Technical improvements support discovery and usability. Rankings and traffic also depend on competition, relevance, original content, reputation and links; a local Lighthouse score does not predict a search position.
