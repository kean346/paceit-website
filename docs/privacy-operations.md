## Retention and requests

- Apply the six-month limit to unsuccessful enquiries in the mailbox and other active business records. Set a review/reminder process so they are deleted by the deadline; the application does not automatically clean the mailbox or Brevo records.
- Classify client, billing and agreement records. Record the applicable legal starting point and any longer tax, audit, dispute or other retention duty before deleting records on a five-year schedule. Do not delete information still needed for an active agreement merely because its creation date is old.
- Monitor `info@paceit.co.za` for privacy requests. Log the receipt date, verify identity proportionately, locate relevant records, handle eligible requests within the stated 30-day target and explain any lawful exception or applicable statutory extension.
- Make deletion requests to providers where appropriate; distinguish active records, backups, security logs and records that must be retained. Do not promise immediate permanent removal from every provider system.

## Providers, transfers and security

- Keep an accurate inventory of Vercel, Brevo, the mailbox host and any additional providers, including their data-processing terms, sub-processors, locations and retention schedules.
- Verify applicable operator agreements and POPIA section 72 safeguards for international transfers. European hosting or a provider’s GDPR statement alone does not establish compliance for paceIT’s use. Retain the relevant agreements and account settings.
- Restrict business access to authorised accounts, use MFA where available, protect server-only SMTP credentials and establish how security incidents will be assessed and handled under applicable law.
- The website currently targets South African businesses and does not implement behavioural profiling, analytics or advertising storage. Reassess GDPR/UK GDPR scope if activities or targeting change; citizenship alone is not the test for applicability.
- Reassess the privacy notice and consent controls before enabling any optional analytics, advertising cookies or persistent visitor tracking.
